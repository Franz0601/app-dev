# My Favorite Series: Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End

## ✨ Overview
**Frieren: Beyond Journey’s End** (*Sousou no Frieren*) is a Japanese fantasy anime and manga series.  
It tells the story of **Frieren**, an elven mage who once traveled with a hero party to defeat the Demon King.  
After their victory, she continues to live for centuries, reflecting on her journey and learning to understand the value of time, emotions, and human connections.

### 🧝‍♀️ Main Characters
- **Frieren** — The immortal elf mage searching for meaning after her companions are gone.  
- **Fern** — A young mage who becomes Frieren’s apprentice.  
- **Stark** — A brave but shy warrior who travels with them.  
- **Heiter** — A kind priest and one of Frieren’s original companions.  
- **Himmel** — The hero of the group, remembered fondly by Frieren.

### 💬 Memorable Quote
> “People forget so easily... even so, I want to remember.”  
> — Frieren

### 🌸 Why I Love This Series
1. It beautifully explores **the passage of time** and **the meaning of memories**.  
2. The art and **music** are calming yet emotional.  
3. It reminds me that even quiet stories can be deeply powerful.
